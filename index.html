Rimuru Tempest

Founder & Demon Lord of Tempest

A former human reborn as a slime who builds the Jura Tempest Federation through allies, diplomacy, and extraordinary power.

Storm Dragon

One of the world’s True Dragons and Rimuru’s closest friend, known for immense strength and a spirited, curious nature.

Veldanava

Star-King Dragon

The eldest True Dragon and a legendary creator figure whose choices shaped the world and the lives of its strongest beings.

Velgrynd

Scorch Dragon & Imperial Empress

A powerful True Dragon of flame and acceleration, devoted to Rudra and deeply tied to the Eastern Empire.

Velzard

White Ice Dragon

An ancient True Dragon whose overwhelming ice power and formidable presence place her among the world’s greatest forces.

Diablo

Primordial Black & Tempest Butler

A primordial demon of exceptional intellect and strength who pledges unwavering loyalty to Rimuru as one of Tempest’s leaders.

Carrera

Primordial Yellow & Tempest Executive

A battle-loving primordial demon recruited by Diablo, feared for her devastating magic and now serving under Rimuru.

Testarossa

Primordial White & Diplomat

The poised Primordial White, recruited into Tempest and entrusted with high-level diplomacy thanks to her sharp judgment and strength.

Hinata Sakaguchi

Holy Knight & Crusader Leader

An elite swordswoman and leader among the Holy Knights whose resolve, skill, and later alliance with Tempest make her pivotal.
